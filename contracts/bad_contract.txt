Clause 1: Deployment Costs
The Worker agrees to pay a placement fee equivalent to two months' salary. This amount will be an immediate deduction from the first month's pay as a security deposit.

Clause 2: Payment Method
Due to administrative constraints, wages may be issued via promissory notes or company vouchers redeemable at designated local stores.

Clause 3: Resignation
If the Worker resigns before the end of the term, they must pay an exit fee of PHP 50,000 to cover processing and deployment costs.
