Clause 1: Remuneration
The Employer shall pay the Helper a salary of HKD 4,870 per month. Wages shall be paid in legal tender at intervals not exceeding one month.

Clause 2: Rest Days
The Helper shall be entitled to at least one rest day in every period of seven days. A rest day is a continuous period of not less than 24 hours.

Clause 3: Termination
Either party may terminate this contract by giving one (1) month’s notice in writing or by paying one (1) month’s wages in lieu of notice.
