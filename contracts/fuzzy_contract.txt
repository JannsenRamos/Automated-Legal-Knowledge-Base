Clause 1: Operational Hours
The Helper agrees to remain on standby for 12 hours daily to ensure household security. Meal breaks will be taken only when duties permit and are not guaranteed to be 60 minutes.

Clause 2: Rest Arrangement
To maximize earnings, the Helper may waive their weekly rest day in exchange for a small daily allowance, provided the employer approves.

Clause 3: Leave Eligibility
Service incentive leave of 5 days shall only be granted after the Worker completes three (3) consecutive years of service with the same employer.
